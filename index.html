🎙️ dB Meter

Privacy Policy — Last updated: March 27, 2026

Overview

dB Meter is a sound level measurement app developed by TheKnack. Your privacy is our priority.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. dB Meter does not require an account, does not use analytics SDKs, does not contain ads, and does not track you in any way.

Microphone Access

The app requests microphone access solely to measure ambient sound levels. Audio is processed in real-time on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Location Data

If you grant location permission, your approximate location is stored locally with your measurement records. Location data never leaves your device.

In-App Purchases

dB Meter uses Apple's StoreKit for optional in-app purchases. No other third-party payment services are used.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
