Privacy Policy

dB Meter — Last updated: March 27, 2026

Overview

dB Meter is a sound level measurement app developed by TheKnack. Your privacy is our priority.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. dB Meter does not require an account, does not use analytics SDKs, does not contain ads, and does not track you in any way.

Microphone Access

The app requests microphone access solely to measure ambient sound levels. Audio is processed in real-time on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Location Data

If you grant location permission, your approximate location is stored locally with your measurement records to help you remember where readings were taken. Location data never leaves your device.

In-App Purchases

dB Meter uses Apple's StoreKit for optional in-app purchases (Pro subscription and lifetime unlock). Payment is processed by Apple. No other third-party payment services are used.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
