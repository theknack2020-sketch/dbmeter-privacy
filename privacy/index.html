dB Meter

Privacy Policy

dB Meter by TheKnack. Updated November 2026, for dB Meter 3.0.

  • dB Meter collects no data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Sound is measured on your device and nothing is uploaded.
  • Audio clips are saved only when you record one, and stay on your device; they can be part of your own device backups.
  • dB Meter does not use your location.

The microphone

dB Meter asks for the microphone so it can measure the sound level around you. It listens only while a measurement runs. The sound is turned into numbers (the level, min, average, max and peak) on your device, in real time, and the sound itself is not saved.

The one exception is an audio clip you choose to record with dB Meter Pro (Record Clip on the Meter, or Settings, Recordings). A clip is saved as an audio file in dB Meter's storage on your device, protected by iOS so it cannot be read while your device is locked. dB Meter never uploads it. You can play, share or delete it in the app; it leaves your device only in your own device backups or if you share it yourself.

If you start a measurement and then lock the screen or switch apps, dB Meter keeps measuring until you stop it, for up to 8 hours, and iOS shows its microphone indicator the whole time. dB Meter never starts the microphone by itself: only you start a measurement, in the app, from a widget, from Control Center or by asking Siri.

What stays on your device

  • Your measurements: date, time, length, average, min and max, and the weighting and calibration used
  • Today's noise dose, your settings, your streak and achievements
  • Audio clips you record (dB Meter Pro)
  • A CSV file, only while you share it: dB Meter deletes it when the share sheet closes
  • While a measurement runs, a short summary every 10 seconds, so it is not lost if iOS closes dB Meter
  • For the widgets: a copy of your latest measurement and today's dose, in an App Group, a storage area on your device that only dB Meter and its widgets can read

dB Meter sends none of this anywhere and has no server. Like all app data, it can be part of your own iPhone or iPad backup if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer.

The widgets and the Live Activity show your readings on your own device. When you ask Siri or Shortcuts, they get only the answer you asked for (the level, or today's dose); a Siri check is saved in your History like any measurement.

Notifications

If you allow them, dB Meter schedules a daily reminder, a reminder when your streak is at risk, and a nudge after a few days without a measurement, all on your device. They are local notifications: no server sends them. Turn the daily reminder off in dB Meter's Settings, or all of them in iOS Settings.

Purchases

dB Meter Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as a monthly or yearly subscription. Apple handles the payment; TheKnack never sees your payment details or your Apple Account. dB Meter checks on your device, with Apple's StoreKit, whether you have dB Meter Pro.

Links that leave the app

Only when you tap them: the Terms of Use and this policy open in an in-app browser and are hosted by GitHub Pages, whose own privacy statement covers your visit; Rate on App Store and the Caliper card open Apple's App Store, Share App opens the share sheet; Contact Support opens your email app.

Children

dB Meter collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a measurement in History (you get a few seconds to undo it), delete a clip in Settings, Recordings, or delete everything with Settings, Reset All Data. Deleting dB Meter deletes all of its data from your device.

Changes

If dB Meter starts handling data differently, this page will say so before the version that does it, with a new date.
