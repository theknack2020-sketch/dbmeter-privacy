Accuracy

How accurate is dB Meter?

Its readings are estimates. The calculations follow the standards: the A- and C-weighting follow the IEC 61672 curves, the meter uses the Fast time weighting, and the average is the true sound energy average (LAeq). The limit is the microphone. Phone microphones differ by model, are tuned for speech, and are affected by cases, covers and where you hold the phone, so dB Meter can read differently from a calibrated sound level meter, most of all at very quiet and very loud levels. To get closer, calibrate it against a reference meter (below).

What do “<30” and OVER mean?

“<30” means the room is quieter than the microphone can measure, so dB Meter does not guess a number. OVER means the sound was too loud for the microphone input and the real level was higher than shown; the summary remembers it.

Is dB Meter a medical device?

No. It is not a medical device or a certified sound level meter, and not a tool for workplace compliance or legal evidence. For those, use a calibrated instrument.

Calibration

How do I calibrate dB Meter?

Calibration is free. Put your phone next to a calibrated sound level meter set to A-weighting, with a steady sound playing. Open Settings, Measurement, Calibration, and move the slider (up to 10 dB either way, in 0.5 dB steps) until dB Meter shows the same level. The meter then says “Calibrated” with your offset under the number. Calibration applies to every new measurement and to today's noise dose.

I plugged in another microphone

dB Meter keeps measuring with it and tells you its readings can differ from the built-in microphone. Calibrate again for that microphone if you need to.

dBA, dBC and dBZ

dBA follows how the ear hears everyday sound and is used for noise exposure. dBC counts more of the low, rumbling sound and is used for loud music and peaks. dBZ has no weighting. Everyone measures in dBA; dBC and dBZ are part of dB Meter Pro (Settings, Measurement, Frequency Weighting). The peak in each summary is C-weighted.

Noise dose

What is today's noise dose?

How much of a day's limit you have used, by the NIOSH method: 85 dBA for 8 hours is 100%, and every 3 dB louder halves the time (88 dBA for 4 hours, 91 dBA for 2 hours). It is free, on the Insights tab, and starts again at midnight.

Like a NIOSH dosimeter, it uses the Slow response, and sound below 80 dBA adds nothing.

Why is my dose lower than I expected?

It counts only the time you measure, also with the screen locked, not the rest of your day. Sound below 80 dBA adds nothing.

What does dB Meter Pro add?

The measured average (LAeq), the 8-hour TWA, the time left today at 85 dBA, and an alert at 50, 80 and 100% of today's dose while you measure, with Keep Measuring and Stop Measuring. On iPhone, when dB Meter is in the background, the alert appears on the Live Activity.

NIOSH or OSHA?

dB Meter uses NIOSH, which is stricter than the OSHA limit in US workplaces (90 dBA for 8 hours, 5 dB exchange rate). Neither turns a phone estimate into a compliance measurement.

Measuring

How do I start?

Tap Start on the Meter tab, and Stop when you are done. You can also tap a dB Meter widget, use the Measure Noise control in Control Center (iOS 18 and later), or say “Measure noise with dB Meter” to Siri. Measurements longer than 2 seconds are saved.

Does it keep measuring with the screen locked?

Yes. A measurement you started keeps running when the screen locks or you switch apps, for up to 8 hours, and iOS shows its microphone indicator. On iPhone a Live Activity shows the level on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; tap Stop there or in the app. iPad keeps measuring without a Live Activity. If iOS ever closes dB Meter during a long run, what was measured up to its last checkpoint (every 10 seconds) is saved the next time you open it.

Why did my measurement stop?

A measurement ends, and is saved, when you tap Stop (in the app or on the Live Activity), after 8 hours, or when a phone call or another app takes the microphone. After an interruption, Measure Again starts a new one.

dB Meter can't hear anything

Allow the microphone in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Microphone, dB Meter. The Meter tab has an Open Settings button when access is off.

Widgets, Siri and Control Center

What do the widgets show?

Your latest measurement with its zone, and today's noise dose, on the Home Screen (small and medium) and the Lock Screen. A tap opens dB Meter, and once setup is finished it starts measuring if the microphone is allowed. Add one from the Home Screen: touch and hold, then Edit, Add Widget, dB Meter.

What can I ask Siri?

“How loud is it with dB Meter?”: dB Meter opens, measures for a few seconds and tells you the level and, in dBA, its zone; the check is saved in History. “What's my noise dose in dB Meter?” answers without opening the app. You can also say “Start measuring with dB Meter” or “Open history in dB Meter”.

Control Center and the Action button

On iOS 18 and later, add the Measure Noise control to Control Center or the Action button. It opens dB Meter, and once setup is finished it starts measuring if the microphone is allowed; stop in the app or on the Live Activity.

History and CSV

How long are measurements kept?

They stay on your device until you delete them. Free shows the last 7 days; dB Meter Pro opens them all. You can delete any measurement, even one you can't open, and undo it for a few seconds.

How do I find a measurement?

Search History by level (“loud”), a dB value (“72”) or a date (“Nov 11”, “yesterday”), or use the date filter.

How do I export CSV?

With dB Meter Pro, tap the share button at the top of History. The file has, for each measurement shown, the date in UTC and in your local time, the length, average, min, max, zone (for dBA), weighting and calibration. If your subscription ended, you can still export what you measured while you had it.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, Reset All Data. It lists what will go (measurements, clips, exports, today's dose, streak and achievements, settings) and cannot be undone. Your subscription is not affected.

Audio clips

With dB Meter Pro, tap Record Clip on the Meter while it is not measuring, or use Settings, Recordings. Clips play in the app and show the measurement taken at the same time, if there is one. They stay on your device and can be part of your own device backups; share or delete them there. If your subscription ends, you can still play, share and delete them.

Free and dB Meter Pro

Free: the live meter in dBA with min, average and max, calibration, today's noise dose, 7 days of history, measuring with the screen locked, widgets, Siri, Control Center and 2 of the 5 themes. No ads.

dB Meter Pro: dBC and dBZ, dose alerts and the NIOSH detail, charts, unlimited history, CSV export, audio clips and all 5 themes. Monthly or yearly; new subscribers get 7 days free on the yearly plan. There are no weekly plans and no ads.

How do I cancel?

Tap Manage Subscription at the top of dB Meter's Settings, or in iOS Settings tap your name, then Subscriptions, then dB Meter Pro. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Restore

I have dB Meter Pro but the app says Free

Tap Restore Purchases on the plan card at the top of dB Meter's Settings (or Restore on the dB Meter Pro screen), signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. dB Meter tells you what it found. Family members can share a monthly or yearly plan through Family Sharing.

I bought Lifetime Pro

Lifetime Pro is no longer sold, and everyone who bought it keeps dB Meter Pro. Restore it the same way; Settings then shows “Lifetime Pro: yours forever”.
