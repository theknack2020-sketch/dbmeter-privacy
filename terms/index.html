2. What the readings are

dB Meter measures sound with your device's microphone. Its readings, including the noise dose, are estimates for information only. A phone microphone is not a calibrated instrument, and readings can differ between devices, cases, positions and microphones.

dB Meter is not a medical device and not a certified sound level meter or noise dosimeter. Do not use it to diagnose or treat anything, to decide whether a workplace meets a legal limit, or as evidence. For those, use a calibrated instrument operated as its standard requires. The noise dose follows the NIOSH method (85 dBA for 8 hours, 3 dB exchange rate) and counts only the time you measure.
